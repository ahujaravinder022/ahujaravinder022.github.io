🧑‍🔬 About Me

Gen AI/ML Leader & AI Researcher

Bridging the gap between cutting-edge AI research and enterprise-scale Gen AI/ML leadership.

I am a Senior Manager at Accenture's Center for Advanced AI, leading Generative AI and agentic AI initiatives, with 20 years of combined experience across academia and the AI/ML industry spanning healthcare, financial services, and enterprise domains. I have architected Gen AI/ML solutions as Senior Solution Architect at HCLTech and managed data science teams as Manager, Data Science at Evalueserve, building on top-tier academic grounding from IIT Roorkee, JIIT, and Galgotias University. My work spans healthcare document intelligence, clinical knowledge retrieval, financial/healthcare policy intelligence, multi-agent systems, LLM fine-tuning, and AI model governance.

I stay hands-on with the fastest-moving part of the field — I hold 14+ professional certifications across agentic AI and Gen AI platforms from Microsoft, OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic, and regularly judge Gen AI hackathons (Code Cubicle 3.0 with Geek Room & Mastercard) and speak on applied Gen AI topics such as Claude Code, prompt engineering, and Gen AI in Fintech.

My doctoral research at IIT Roorkee focused on Natural Language Processing — specifically sarcasm detection, sentiment analysis, and opinion mining — leveraging state-of-the-art transformer architectures including BERT, BERTweet, and BiGRU attention models. I publish in top-tier SCI-E and Scopus-indexed journals and have received recognition including a Best Paper Award at Springer's international conference on Artificial Intelligence and Evolutionary Computations. My work is cited 1562+ times with an h-index of 16 on Google Scholar.

Beyond research and industry leadership, I have delivered expert talks at IIT Roorkee, Thapar University, KR Mangalam University, Amity University, NITTTR Chandigarh, and GJU Hisar, and led Faculty Development Programmes on Generative AI, Azure ML, and Data Science for universities across India.
