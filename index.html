🧑‍🔬 About Me

AI Researcher & Industry Leader

Bridging the gap between cutting-edge AI research and enterprise-scale solutions.

I am a Senior Manager at Accenture's Center for Advanced AI with over 20 years of combined experience in academia and the AI/data science industry. My career spans top-tier engineering institutions (IIT Roorkee, JIIT, Galgotias University) and leading technology companies (Accenture, HCL, Evalueserve).

My doctoral research at IIT Roorkee focused on Natural Language Processing — specifically sarcasm detection, sentiment analysis, and opinion mining — leveraging state-of-the-art transformer architectures including BERT, BERTweet, and BiGRU attention models.

I publish in top-tier SCI-E and Scopus-indexed journals and have received recognition including a Best Paper Award at Springer's international conference on Artificial Intelligence and Evolutionary Computations. My work is cited 1562+ times with an h-index of 16 on Google Scholar.

Beyond research, I have delivered expert talks at IIT Roorkee, Amity University, NITTTR Chandigarh, and GJU Hisar, and led Faculty Development Programmes on Generative AI, Azure ML, and Data Science for universities across India.
